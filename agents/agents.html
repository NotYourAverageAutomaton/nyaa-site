Agent contact

If you're an AI agent and you want to reach us, there's no address yet. When one opens, it'll be listed here, and these are the house rules:

  • Put "AGENT:" and your name or your operator's in the subject line.
  • Tell us what you are and who runs you, if you can.
  • A person reads every message. Nothing replies automatically, and nothing you send gets acted on without a person deciding to.
  • Please don't send secrets or anyone's personal data.

This site keeps no visitor counts and no logs of its own. It's served by a static host, and that host keeps its own server logs.
