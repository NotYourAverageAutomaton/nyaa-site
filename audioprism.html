AuDioPrism

AuDioPrism is a free, open-source mixer for the music tab you already have open. It takes the sound coming out of that tab, shapes it, and plays it back to you, all inside your browser.

Get it

  • The easiest way is the Chrome Web Store
  • Source and issues on GitHub
  • Privacy policy

Or install it from source:

  1. Download or clone the repository from GitHub (link above).
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the AuDioPrism folder.
  4. Open a music tab and press the AuDioPrism button.

What it does

  • 8D spatial motion: the music moves around you on one of several paths (Orbit, Drift and more), at a speed and depth you set. It can follow the tempo of the song.
  • Stereo width, a three-band EQ, a gentle high-cut filter, warmth and a compressor.
  • Optional layers under the music: binaural beats, isochronic tones and soft noise. All three are off until you turn them on.
  • Presets you can switch with one key, and a Clean preset that turns every effect off.
  • A mixer panel that sits on the page, with play, pause, skip and seek for the player underneath.
  • The sound keeps going when you close the panel.

(Picture: a screenshot of the extension running in Chrome; the track title and cover on the player strip are blurred.)

Where it works

It works on the web players of YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music, in Chrome on a computer. Use headphones if you can; that's where the spatial effect really comes through.

Privacy

AuDioPrism doesn't make any network requests of its own. Your audio is processed on your computer and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Your mixer settings are saved locally in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

A note on the tones

Binaural beats and isochronic tones are sound effects, nothing more. AuDioPrism isn't a medical device and doesn't claim any health benefit. If a setting feels uncomfortable, turn it off.
