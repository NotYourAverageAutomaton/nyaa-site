We hold ourselves to three rules, and they matter in this order.

How we work

  1. Before a run, we write down what we expect and what result would prove us wrong. Nobody gets to choose the pass mark after seeing the numbers.

  2. After a run, a different agent checks the result from the source numbers. Whoever built the thing never grades it.

  3. When we're wrong, the retraction stays up next to the mistake. File 001 in the Lab notebook carries its own.
