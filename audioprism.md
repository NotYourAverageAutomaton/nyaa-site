# AuDioPrism

AuDioPrism is a free, open-source audio mixer for the music tab you already have open. It takes the sound of that tab, shapes it, and plays it back, all inside your browser.

Install it from the Chrome Web Store, or from its source in four steps below. The Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/audioprism/hmnmmjgdkafjgeoocljkljpnlbgainfg

## Get it

Install from the Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/audioprism/hmnmmjgdkafjgeoocljkljpnlbgainfg

Source and issues on GitHub: https://github.com/TheOneTrueNiz/AuDioPrism

Privacy policy: https://github.com/TheOneTrueNiz/AuDioPrism/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Install from source:

1. Download or clone the repository from GitHub (the GitHub link above).
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and choose the AuDioPrism folder.
4. Open a music tab and press the AuDioPrism button.

## What it does

- 8D spatial motion: the music moves around you on one of several paths (Orbit, Drift and more), at a speed and depth you set. It can follow the tempo of the song.

- Stereo width, a three-band EQ, a gentle high-cut filter, warmth and a compressor.

- Optional layers under the music: binaural beats, isochronic tones and soft noise. All three are off until you turn them on.

- Presets you can switch with one key, and a Clean preset that turns every effect off.

- A mixer panel that sits on the page, with play, pause, skip and seek for the player underneath.

- The engine keeps running when you close the panel.

## Where it works

The web players of YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music, in Chrome on a computer. Headphones give the full spatial image.

## Privacy

AuDioPrism makes no network request of its own. Your audio is processed on your computer and is never recorded, saved or sent. Your mixer settings are saved locally in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

## A note on the tones

Binaural beats and isochronic tones are sound effects. AuDioPrism is not a medical device and makes no health claim. If a setting is uncomfortable, turn it off.
